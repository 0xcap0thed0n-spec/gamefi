# Vision

## Collection

- **555 Genesis** characters at launch.
- Later expansion of **5,000** more for a capped **5,555** total.

## Inspirations (what we're borrowing)

| Project | Takeaway |
|---------|----------|
| **DeFi Kingdoms** | GameFi loop — NFTs as characters/assets inside an economy, not just JPEGs |
| **The Remnants / The Heist** | Narrative + heist/adventure framing around collectible characters |
| **Dead Pixels Ghost Club** | Pack / forever-mint mechanics that keep minting interesting even on a smaller chain (Hedera) |

## Utility pillars

1. **Trading / portfolio dashboard** — see what you hold, performance, and collection context.
2. **GameFi** — characters participate in gameplay and (eventually) economy loops.

## Principles

- Ship **utility before final art** — placeholders and testnet are enough to learn.
- Keep mint economics and pack odds **documentable and auditable**.
- Prefer one clear monorepo until the team/stack forces a split.
